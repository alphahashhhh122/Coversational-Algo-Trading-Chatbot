How to use this platform — step by step
  1. Research — ask the chat anything: "price of Reliance", "put-call ratio for Nifty", "analyze TCS fundamentally", "find stocks where RSI is below 30".
  2. Create a strategy — describe it in plain English in the chat (e.g. "Create a Reliance 5 minute strategy that buys when EMA 9 crosses above EMA 21 with a 2% stop loss"), then open Strategies to review and save it.
  3. Backtest — just ask, e.g. "backtest an EMA crossover on HDFCBANK". History is fetched automatically — no manual import needed. You'll see trades, P&L, drawdown, and the equity curve.
  4. Trade — tell the chat the order, e.g. "buy 10 RELIANCE at market" or "sell 5 TCS". The assistant prepares it and asks you to confirm — reply approve and it goes to your broker. You can also "square off everything" or "cancel all orders".
  5. See results — the Account tab shows your funds, positions, orders, and trades synced from the broker.

Paper mode is the default and uses simulated money. Live trading only activates behind risk checks and your explicit approval — the banner at the top of this page tells you which mode you're in.
