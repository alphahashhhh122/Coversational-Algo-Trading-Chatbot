How to use this platform — step by step
  1. Research — ask the chat anything: "price of Reliance", "put-call ratio for Nifty", "analyze TCS fundamentally", "find stocks where RSI is below 30".
  2. Create a strategy — describe it in plain English in the chat (e.g. "Create a Reliance 5 minute strategy that buys when EMA 9 crosses above EMA 21 with a 2% stop loss"), then open Strategies to review and save it.
  3. Backtest — open Research, pick your dataset and strategy, press Run backtest. You'll see trades, P&L, drawdown, and the equity curve. Choose mode Semi-auto paper prep when you want to trade the signals.
  4. Paper trade — open Execution: select the run and its risk decision, press Prepare intent, then Approve it (you are the human in the loop), then Submit. The order goes to your broker's Analyzer (paper) account.
  5. See results — the Monitor tab shows your funds, positions, orders, and trades synced from the broker — and the same order appears live in OpenAlgo's own dashboard at 127.0.0.1:5000 (Orderbook / Analyzer view). Emergency buttons there let you cancel all orders or square off everything.

Paper mode is the default and uses simulated money. Live trading only activates behind risk checks and your explicit approval — the red LIVE banner in Execution tells you which mode you're in.
